Three steps to watch

The app installs empty by design. Your content comes from your own playlist.

1

Open Settings

Launch the app and go to Settings. There is nothing preloaded — you are in control of what is added.

2

Add your M3U URL

Paste a valid M3U / M3U8 playlist URL and set the region. Create as many playlist profile sets as you like.

3

Watch, guide & cast

Browse channels by category, check the EPG guide, star favourites, and cast the stream to your TV.

FAQ

Quick answers before you install.

Does the app include TV channels?

No. IPTV is a standalone player only. It ships with no channels, streams, or guide data. You add your own M3U / M3U8 playlist URL from a source you are licensed to use.

Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free to install on Android. It contains ads and offers in-app purchases.

What do I need to start watching?

A valid M3U or M3U8 playlist URL from your IPTV provider, plus an internet connection. An XMLTV EPG URL is optional, for the program guide.

Does it work on tablets and phones?

Yes. The interface is built on Material 3 with a dark theme tuned for both Android phones and tablets.

Can I watch on my TV?

Yes — cast live streams to Chromecast / Google Cast devices, with playback and volume control from the phone.

Is there a parental control?

Yes. Lock settings and restricted actions behind a password / PIN, restrict the channel view to favourites only, and use child lock to pin playback to the current channel.

Will it save mobile data?

Data saver mode caps stream quality to SD (480p / 1 Mbps), auto-saver can trigger on cellular, and the app shows your monthly Wi-Fi and cellular streaming totals.

Ready to watch your playlist?

Free on Google Play. Bring your own M3U / M3U8 playlist.
