How it works

Three steps per fill-up

Logging takes less time than pumping the tank.

  1. 1

    Snap the receipt

    Take a photo, or upload one from your gallery. Prefer typing? Log manually — that path is always free.

  2. 2

    Review what it read

    Each detected item appears as a card: fuel, coffee, loyalty discount. Tap the trash to drop anything that is not fuel.

  3. 3

    Confirm the odometer

    Add the current odometer reading and FuelFlow does the maths: distance, cost per km, km/L or MPG.

Auto-calculated distance = odometer − last odometer cost / km = total ÷ distance km / L = distance ÷ litres

FAQ

Common questions

Will the receipt scanner work with my receipts?

It is tested against Australian, US and UK printed fuel receipts. It should work with any printed receipt as long as the photo is clear and the details are printed on it. If a scan misses something, you can correct it before saving.

Do I need an internet connection to log fuel?

No. Manual logging is always available, and the on-device scanner runs entirely on your phone. The online AI scanner is the option that needs a connection.

Where does my data go?

Your logs live on your phone. Local backup writes to your device; Google Drive backup writes to your own Drive account. There are no ads and no sale of your driving data.

Is km/L and MPG both supported?

Yes. FuelFlow calculates distance from consecutive odometer readings and reports economy in km/L or MPG, plus cost per km, in the units you use.

Can I manage more than one vehicle?

Yes. Add cars, trucks and motorcycles, switch between them, and keep separate expense records for each. Summary stats are per vehicle.

Can I get my data out of the app?

Yes — export CSV reports of your fuel expenses (premium), and back up locally or to Google Drive so you can restore on a new device.

What exactly is free, and what is premium?

Manual logging, stats, charts, multi-vehicle use and local backup are free. Receipt scanning, CSV export and Google Drive backup are premium. You can scan 5 receipts free to see how the scanner works.
